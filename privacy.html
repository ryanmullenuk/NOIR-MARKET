Privacy policy

Updated 8 September 2026. Applies to Noir Market version 1.0 for iOS and the Redhead Games support pages.

Who is responsible

Noir Market is developed by Ryan Mullen under the Redhead Games name. For privacy questions or requests, email ryanmullenuk@yahoo.co.uk.

Game progress and settings

The iOS app stores game progress, your chosen player name and avatar, and sound and music preferences on your device. These are not uploaded to a Redhead Games server. The app has no registration, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or online leaderboard, and does not request access to your contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Local data remains until it is replaced or removed. Deleting the app can delete that data. Device backups are controlled by your device settings and Apple; the game does not provide its own cloud-save or cross-device sync service. In the browser edition, the equivalent information is stored in your browser and can be removed by clearing that website's data.

Apple purchases and diagnostics

Apple processes in-app payments. The app checks Apple's verified purchase-entitlement information to determine whether all cities are unlocked. Redhead Games does not receive payment-card details or your Apple account password through the app. Restore purchases checks purchase ownership; it does not restore game progress.

Apple may process purchase information and diagnostics under its own policies and your settings, including during TestFlight testing. See Apple's privacy information. No separate crash-reporting or analytics service is built into this app.

If you contact support

If you email support, Ryan Mullen receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. This information is used to respond, investigate faults and manage your request. The basis for this processing is the legitimate interest in providing support and maintaining the game.

Support correspondence is handled through the Yahoo email service and is kept only for as long as needed to resolve the enquiry, deal with related follow-up or meet applicable legal obligations. Please avoid including unnecessary personal or sensitive information. Redhead Games does not use your messages for advertising or sell them. Yahoo's own processing is described in its privacy policy.

Public website pages

The support and privacy pages are hosted using GitHub Pages. Visiting them makes a normal web request to the hosting provider, which may process technical information such as your IP address for delivery and security. These pages do not add analytics, advertising cookies or tracking scripts. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Apple, Yahoo and GitHub operate services internationally, so information handled by those providers may be processed outside the UK under their applicable privacy terms and safeguards. Opening an external link is subject to that provider's privacy policy.

Your choices and rights

You can use Free Play without creating a game account or contacting support. Local game data can be removed by deleting the app or clearing the browser edition's website data. Purchase records held by Apple are managed separately.

For personal information held in support correspondence, you can contact us to request access, correction or deletion, or to object to or request restriction of processing. Other rights, including data portability, may apply depending on the circumstances. We may need to verify your identity before handling a request. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. The date above identifies the latest version.
