Noir Market support

For help with Noir Market, contact Ryan Mullen at Redhead Games.

ryanmullenuk@yahoo.co.uk

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and what happened. A screenshot and the steps that caused the problem can help. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or Apple sign-in codes.

Free Play and city access

Free Play includes London, Manchester and Birmingham. In the iOS edition, one non-consumable in-app purchase unlocks all 14 cities and their travel and shipping routes. There is no subscription. Check the price displayed by Apple before confirming a purchase.

Restore an iOS purchase

Use the Apple account that made the purchase, connect to the internet and tap Restore purchases at the bottom of the app. Restoring recovers the city unlock, not your saved game. The website's preview unlock does not grant an App Store purchase.

Saved games

Progress is stored on your device. The game does not provide an online save account or cross-device game sync. Deleting the app or clearing website data can remove progress. Browser saves do not automatically transfer into the iPhone app.

Offline play

The iOS game includes its game files for offline play. Purchasing, restoring purchases and opening these support pages can require an internet connection.

Purchase or refund queries

Apple handles App Store payments. You can review purchases and request a refund through Apple's purchase-support service. Redhead Games cannot access your payment-card details.

Noir Market is a fictional strategy game. It does not sell real-world goods or offer real-money rewards.
